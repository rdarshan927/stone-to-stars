# Observatory artwork

User-supplied AI-generated decorative illustrations. These are not NASA
photographs, live satellite imagery, climate measurements, or depictions of the
selected location. They must remain separate from scientific chart data.

Optimized from the supplied `earth_globe.png` (1448 × 1086, RGB) and
`earth_horizon.png` (1672 × 941, RGB). Neither source has transparency.

- `earth_globe.webp`: 960 × 720, WebP quality 86.
- `earth_horizon.webp`: 1440 × 810, WebP quality 85.

Resized proportionally with libvips; metadata stripped. Edge blending, cropping,
and the precipitation tint are presentation effects applied in Flutter. Original
artwork is retained in the supplied design directory, outside the application.
